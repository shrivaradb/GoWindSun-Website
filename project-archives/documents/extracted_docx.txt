INTRODUCTION
India: A Global Clean Energy Powerhouse
India is one of the world's fastest-growing major economies and among the most dynamic energy markets globally. With sustained economic growth, rapid industrialization, urbanization, digitalization, and rising electricity demand, India is committed to ensuring affordable, reliable, sustainable, and modern energy access for all.
Today, India is the world's:
3rd largest energy consumer
3rd largest producer of renewable energy globally
4th largest installed renewable energy market
Among the fastest-growing solar and wind energy markets in the world
India has made remarkable progress in clean energy deployment, with renewable energy capacity (including large hydro) crossing 220 GW and continuing to expand rapidly through utility-scale, rooftop, hybrid, storage, and green hydrogen projects.
At the COP26 Summit, India announced its ambitious "Panchamrit" commitments, including achieving 500 GW of non-fossil fuel electricity capacity and meeting nearly 50% of its electricity requirements from non-fossil fuel sources. Building on this vision, the Government of India has further strengthened its commitment by targeting approximately 500 GW of renewable energy capacity by 2030 and achieving Net Zero emissions by 2070.
Progressive policies, competitive auctions, Green Open Access regulations, Renewable Purchase Obligations (RPO), Green Energy Corridor initiatives, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), Pumped Hydro Storage, Green Hydrogen, and the growing participation of commercial and industrial consumers are creating unprecedented opportunities for investors, developers, manufacturers, and technology providers.
India's energy transition represents one of the largest and most attractive clean energy investment opportunities in the world.

What is Open Access Power Procurement?
Open Access allows eligible electricity consumers to purchase power directly from renewable energy generators instead of procuring their entire electricity requirement from the local distribution utility.
Through Open Access arrangements, consumers can:
Reduce electricity procurement costs.
Achieve long-term tariff certainty.
Meet ESG and sustainability targets.
Reduce carbon footprint.
Fulfil Renewable Purchase Obligations (RPOs), where applicable.
Enhance competitiveness through lower energy costs.

Our Journey
GoWindSun’s roots go back to 2014, when the company was incorporated in Pune as GoWind-n-Sun EnerTechCon India Private Limited. Public company records identify the company as an active private limited company involved in electric power generation.
The company’s earlier business material established its focus around wind, solar, technology and consultancy, with a stated objective of supporting India’s demand for clean and sustainable energy.
Today, the vision can evolve further towards renewable energy project development, investment and IPP opportunities, while retaining the technical and industry expertise developed over the years.

Homepage hero — my recommended version
I would not put too much text on the homepage. The first screen should be visually strong, using your GoWindSun logo and a high-quality wind + solar image.
Headline:
Powering India’s Clean Energy Future
Subheadline:
Developing wind, solar, hybrid and energy storage solutions for a cleaner, more sustainable India.
Buttons:
Explore Our Solutions | Partner With Us
Then immediately below:
Wind | Solar | Hybrid | BESS | Open Access | IPP
This positioning gives GoWindSun a much stronger identity than presenting it simply as a renewable-energy consultancy. It also leaves room for the company to grow into a substantial IPP and renewable energy asset owner.
One important point: I would not publish specific MW figures, project achievements, client names, investor names, certifications or “years of experience” claims on the website until we verify the exact information. That will make the website credible to investors and C&I customers rather than looking promotional.
