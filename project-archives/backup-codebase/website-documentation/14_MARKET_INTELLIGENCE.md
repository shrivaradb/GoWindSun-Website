# GoWindSun Website — Market Intelligence Specification

This document details the clean energy market intelligence requirements, technology sectors, pricing benchmarks, tender tracking criteria, and the exact 14 target countries monitored across the platform.

---

## 1. Primary Focus Sectors & Benchmarks

### 1.1 Wind Energy Sector
* **Onshore Wind:** Turbine ratings (3.3MW to 6.X MW platforms), hub heights, capacity factors, balance of plant (BOP) civil costs.
* **Offshore Wind:** Fixed-bottom and floating offshore wind arrays, foundation supply (monopiles/jackets), subsea inter-array cabling.
* **Wind Turbine Prices:** WTG equipment supply packages, bidding benchmarks, OEM quotes.
* **Wind Project CAPEX:** Total installed project CAPEX (per kW / MW).

### 1.2 Solar PV Sector
* **Solar PV Equipment:** N-Type TOPCon panels, Heterojunction (HJT) modules, string & central inverters, single-axis trackers.
* **Solar Projects:** Ground-mounted utility parks, floating solar PV, industrial rooftop.
* **Solar PV Prices:** FOB export module prices ($/W), delivered DDP prices.
* **Solar Project CAPEX:** Turnkey EPC project costs (e.g., ₹3.45 Cr/MW in India, $1.08M/MW in US).

### 1.3 Tenders & Procurement Sector
* **Tenders & Auctions:** Utility-scale PPA auctions, tariff bidding rounds, ISTS tenders.
* **Auction Scope:** MW/GW capacity offered, tender authority (SECI, BNetzA, DESNZ, CRE, SPPC, EWEC, AEMO), winning tariffs, contract durations (15-30 years).
* **Procurement Benchmarks:** Strike prices, feed-in premiums, CfDs.
