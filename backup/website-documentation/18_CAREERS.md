## 2. Eight Core Specialization Domains

The application programme invites candidates across 8 key engineering and commercial domains:

1. **Wind Energy:** Project development, WTG technology, civil/electrical engineering, O&M, commercial structuring.
2. **Solar Energy:** Utility-scale project development, engineering, turnkey EPC, plant operations.
3. **Power Systems:** Grid integration, EHV transmission, distribution, system planning.
4. **Renewable Energy Markets:** Open Access wheeling, bilateral PPAs, power trading, C&I off-take.
5. **Regulatory & Policy:** CERC/MERC, MNRE guidelines, Grid Code compliance, Open Access regulations.
6. **Energy Storage & Hybrids:** Utility BESS, hybrid plant co-location, Round-the-Clock (RTC) RE power.
7. **Project Finance & Valuation:** Project finance modeling, asset valuation, investment underwriting.
8. **Digitalisation & Tech:** SCADA data analytics, digital twins, emerging clean tech software.

---

## 3. Candidate Profiles & Submission Process

### 3.1 Eligibility Categories
* **Young Professionals:** Engineers, MBAs, energy researchers beginning their careers with strong clean energy passion.
* **Mid-Career Professionals:** Experts with practical utility, IPP, EPC, consulting, regulatory, or financial experience.

### 3.2 Submission Desk
* **Application Email:** `director@gowindsun.com`
* **Required Documentation:** Curriculum Vitae (CV) / Professional Profile plus a brief cover note indicating primary specialization area and career background.
* **Review Process:** Applications are reviewed by GoWindSun executive management on a rolling basis.
